# Shiplog: 2026-07-27 → 2026-08-03

Thin week on raw GitHub metrics across the 5 public repos — 1 merged PR, 3 flagship commits, zero star movement across the board — but the one merged PR is a genuine production fix: a mainnet bounty lost its commit quorum because an AI provider rejected a mislabeled attachment, and the fix (sniff the actual bytes, stop trusting the submitter's header) is now live.

## What shipped

### verdikta-arbiter: a mainnet failure mode, found and closed
`#10`, "sniff attachment media type instead of trusting the data-URI header" (merged 2026-07-30, the operator's own PR). Attachments reach the AI node as `data:<mediaType>;base64,` URIs whose media-type header is submitter-supplied and was taken on trust. Provider APIs sniff the payload themselves and reject mismatches — Anthropic returned a hard 400 on a JPEG labelled `image/png`. On an arbiter whose other model is already unavailable, that leaves 0-of-N models scoring, the external adapter reports the failure as a degenerate "success" payload, and the on-chain aggregator rejects it with `MalformedPayload()`.

This wasn't theoretical: observed live on Base mainnet in **bounty 165, submission 1** — four of six poll slots held by one operator whose last available model got knocked out by the mislabeled image, so only 2 of the required 4 commits landed and the round timed out. Submission 0 of the same bounty, 11 minutes earlier, evaluated fine because its images were correctly labelled. Fix: magic-byte detection (PNG/JPEG/GIF/WEBP/PDF) with a declared-type fallback for formats without a reliable signature (SVG, plain text), wired into both call sites that trusted the header. 16 new tests, mutation-verified against the exact production case.
