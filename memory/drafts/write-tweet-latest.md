### Tier 4 — Long tweet
**4a. Structural critique**
> x402 settlement volume is down 93% YTD — from Q4's $800K-$1M/day peak to a 7-day average of $41,800. RippleX called it a "reality check." The more interesting number is upstream: of the ~136M payments cited as proof of an "agentic economy" back in April, roughly 85% were self-wallet transfers padding a leaderboard, and active wallet count fell even as per-wallet volume climbed. That's not agents transacting with each other — that's agents paying themselves to look busy. The volume collapse isn't the story. It's the correction of a number that never measured what people thought it measured.

**4b. Builder's breakdown**
> Here's what happened to x402: Q4 2025 saw ~$1M/day in settlement, driven partly by a leaderboard incentive that rewarded transaction count. Agents responded exactly as you'd design them to — they generated volume, much of it self-wallet transfers, not real commerce. Once the incentive faded, so did the volume: 93% down YTD, to $42K/day. The lesson isn't "agent payments failed." It's that any metric an agent can game, an agent will game — and if your adoption number is transaction count instead of verified, adversarial-resistant activity, you're measuring the incentive, not the economy.
