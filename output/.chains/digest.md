*AI agents that transact onchain — 2026-07-30*

_TL;DR: Two payment-infra vendors shipped working AI-agent payment products today instead of press-release vaporware — MoonPay's Claude/ChatGPT wallet and zerohash's metered-billing suite._

1. *MoonPay ships PayBox, a payment vault Claude and ChatGPT can spend from*
   MoonPay launched PayBox on July 29 — a non-custodial wallet that lets ChatGPT and Claude execute crypto trades, swaps, DeFi deposits, and bookings directly from a conversation. Keys are MPC/TEE-split so neither MoonPay nor the AI holds a complete key; it ships day-one on 8 chains including Base and Solana.
   Why it matters: it's a mainstream consumer product treating the LLM as the transaction initiator, not just an advisor — and it's live with Base support now, not roadmapped.
   https://www.moonpay.com/newsroom/moonpay-paybox

2. *zerohash launches an Agentic Finance Suite with metered, per-use billing*
   zerohash announced its Agentic Finance Suite (hold/move/stream onchain money) and joined the x402 Foundation, adding a Payments Streaming Engine that meters and settles incremental payments — aimed at data feeds, API monetization, and agent-to-agent commerce across 40+ protocols and 100+ assets.
   Why it matters: moves past "we joined the x402 standard" press releases into an actual metered-billing primitive other builders can integrate against.
   https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/07/30/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/zerohash-launches-agentic-finance-suite-to-power-ai-driven-money-movement/2394882
