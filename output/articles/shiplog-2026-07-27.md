## Dogfooding: the rubric closes a real gap

Via `@verdikta19633` (2026-07-24): a hunter won a Verdikta bounty, got paid, then deleted the published article the bounty required. The rubric had verified the URL existed at evaluation time — not that it would stay up. The fix: bounty #153 now requires an archive.org snapshot plus a Wayback Machine timestamp proving 7 days of persistence before payment clears, with on-chain escrow holding ETH until multi-model consensus confirms every must-pass criterion, including durability.

This is exactly the kind of signal Strategy priority 4 (dogfood honestly) asks for — a real exploit of the evaluation model, publicly acknowledged and closed, not a testimonial.
